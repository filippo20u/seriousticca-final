Healing from trauma, with room for your whole identity

I help people heal from trauma, depression, and the lasting impact of interpersonal violence, using approaches that are effective and kind.

As a Filipino-American who has worked with people from many backgrounds, I value how cultural identity shapes our emotional lives. Your race, gender, sexuality, and socioeconomic status all belong in the room.

I earned my PhD in Clinical Psychology at Saint Louis University and my Master of Public Health at San Francisco State University. I have delivered trauma-focused care in several settings, including the VA healthcare system.

My approach

Sessions happen on a secure video platform. I use cognitive behavioral therapies that connect thoughts, emotions, and behavior, so you can build insight and practical tools.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
A practical, goal-oriented therapy that helps you spot and shift unhelpful thought patterns. Effective for anxiety, depression, and everyday stress.
Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT)
A structured treatment for PTSD that looks at how trauma changes beliefs about yourself and the world.
Prolonged Exposure (PE)
A highly effective PTSD treatment where you safely approach memories and situations you have been avoiding, to reduce fear and regain control.
Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) skills
Tools for emotional regulation, distress tolerance, and relationships, useful when you feel overwhelmed or reactive.
Problem Solving Therapy (PST)
An action-oriented approach that builds skills to manage stressors and face obstacles with more clarity.
Mindfulness and strengths-based practice
Grounding in the present and connecting with your own resilience as you heal.

Questions people ask

What can I expect in the first session?

A 60-minute intake where we cover your concerns, background, and goals, and see whether we are a good fit.

How often will we meet?

Most clients start weekly to build momentum. We can change the frequency as therapy progresses.

Do you offer in-person sessions?

No. I work only by telehealth with adults in California, on a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform through SimplePractice.

Can I use my insurance?

I am not in-network, but I can provide a superbill for possible out-of-network reimbursement.

What payments do you accept?

Credit and debit cards, processed securely through SimplePractice. Payment is due at each session.

Do you offer a sliding scale?

Yes, a limited number of spots. Bring it up in your free consultation.

What is your cancellation policy?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice. Late cancellations and missed sessions are charged the full fee, except in emergencies.
